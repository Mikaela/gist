# Crivit Running Light

- IAN: `451124`
- Max 200 lumens.
- [Lidl Self Service FI](https://asiakaspalvelu.lidl.fi/SelfServiceFI/s/product2?productId=01t6N000004IpRzQAK)
- [Full Manual (GB/FI/SE/PL/LT/DE/AT/CH)](https://media.sit-connect.com/public/articlemanual/e9fcd93c-7d13-49ba-bc81-e328355bb4dc.pdf)
  ([web.archive.org](https://web.archive.org/web/*/https://media.sit-connect.com/public/articlemanual/e9fcd93c-7d13-49ba-bc81-e328355bb4dc.pdf))

In 2024 I enqueried Lidl Customer Support on what is the power of the light,
since it doesn't read in the packaging or manual (and I care about light
pollution). They took a while to find out, but the answer is _**max 200
lumens**_.

If you are the person who found this information for me [_tapausnumero
#106409368_] on my birthday, thank you and sorry that I never thanked you in
email. I never had the mental health to and then so much later it would have
felt too awkward.

The light is very helpful for woofwalking when the woof decides to go to spots
that poor street light leaves invisible due to causing so many shadows and
also helps with the nice areas that don't have streetlights. I always use the
less bright option, which the manual says to be 50 % mode and thus _100
lumens_, since at dark the minimum light that allows you to see is the best
and also saves battery.

The manual does reveal that when the 100%/200 lumen mode the rear light
flashes green and in 50%/100 lumen mode it goes stroboscopic. That might
bother me if I ever saw it from the behind.
