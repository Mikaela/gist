## True Wireless Stereo (TWS)

On the Bluetooth button:

> - Press briefly to pair two units before they connect to an external
>   Bluetooth® playback device. A signal tone will be heard if pairing has
>   been carried out successfully. The two speakers now work as stereo
>   speakers.
> - Press briefly press again to break the current TWS connection, a signal
>   tone will be heard.

Later on a dedicated section:

> ## TWS pairing

> **NOTE:** The TWS (True Wireless Stereo) function is only available if two
> of these products have been purchased and both have the same model number.
>
> - Turn on both products and make sure they are both not connected to another
>   device via Bluetooth®.
> - Press the [BT] button [3] on one of the products which will then by
>   default set it as the “primary“ unit. A beep will sound. When pairing
>   between both products has been carried out successfully, the LED [1] of
>   the connected “secondary“ unit stops flashing and a sequence of beeps will
>   sound.
> - The “primary“ unit LED [1] will still be flashing waiting to pair with the
>   playback device. Once the “primary unit“ has successfully paired with the
>   playback device both the “primary“ and “secondary“ units can be played and
>   controlled synchronously during Bluetooth® playback mode.
> - Press the [BT] button [3] again to break the TWS pairing connection, the
>   LED [1] on the connected “secondary“ unit flashes and a signal tone
>   sequence sounds.
>
>   **NOTE:** The product which had its button [3] pressed first will become
>   the “primary“ unit (in the left audio channel), and the other unit will
>   then by default becone the “secondary“ unit (in the right audio channnel).
